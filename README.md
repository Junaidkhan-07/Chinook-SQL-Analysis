# Chinook SQL Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive SQL analysis of the **Chinook Music Store Database** using MySQL.

The analysis focuses on customers, invoices, sales performance, tracks, artists, genres, and customer purchasing behavior. A total of **30 business-focused SQL queries** were developed to extract meaningful insights from the database.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and customer performance
* Identify top-spending customers
* Analyze sales by country
* Identify top-performing tracks and genres
* Analyze monthly sales trends
* Compare customer purchasing behavior
* Apply advanced SQL techniques for business analysis

## 📈 Key Results

### Overall Business Performance

| Metric                |    Result |
| --------------------- | --------: |
| Total Customers       |        59 |
| Total Invoices        |       412 |
| Total Revenue         | $2,328.60 |
| Average Invoice Value |     $5.65 |
| Highest Invoice       |    $25.86 |
| Lowest Invoice        |     $0.99 |

### Top Customer

**Helena Holý** generated the highest total spending among customers with **$49.62**.

### Sales by Country

The **United States** generated the highest sales with **$523.06**, followed by:

* Canada — $303.96
* France — $195.10
* Brazil — $190.10
* Germany — $156.48

### Revenue by Genre

**Rock** generated the highest genre revenue at **$826.65**, followed by:

* Latin — $382.14
* Metal — $261.36
* Alternative & Punk — $241.56

### Monthly Sales

The analysis covered monthly sales from **January 2021 through December 2025**.

The monthly results show a generally consistent recurring sales pattern, with several higher-revenue months such as:

* January 2022 — $52.62
* April 2023 — $51.62
* June 2023 — $50.62
* November 2025 — $49.62
